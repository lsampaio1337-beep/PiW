# Kalos Path Update Report

## 1. Routes & Pokemon Distribution (Sheet 1)
- **Constraint Enforcement:** Every single route strictly contains between **3 and 9 Pokemon**.
- **Starter Depots:** Created `Starter Depot Spot (Kalos)` and `Starter Depot Spot (Kanto)`. I placed the Base and Stage 1 evolutions of all starters in these spots (e.g., Bulbasaur, Ivysaur, Chespin, Quilladin). I split them into two spots to avoid exceeding the 9 Pokemon limit. They also feature 1 Ultra Rare Pokemon each to bulk them up.
- **Post-League Halls:** The Legendaries were grouped as requested and merged into 3 specific ultimate halls:
    - **Sea Spirit Den Hall:** Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres
    - **Team Flare Secret HQ Hall:** Xerneas, Yveltal, Zygarde
    - **Ultimate Chamber:** Mewtwo, Hoopa, Volcanion, Diancie
- **No 1-Pokemon Routes:** Since these Legendary Halls would only have 3-4 Pokemon, they were bundled with 2 additional "Ultra Rare" normal Pokemon to diversify the route.
- **Level Scaling:** The main route sequence (ending at Kalos Pokemon League) scales from Level 1 up to Level 70. Post-Game locations scale from 70 to exactly 100.

## 2. Challenges (Sheet 2)
- **Main Challenge Sequence:** All main game routes (including Gyms explicitly modeled as official challenges) are integrated into a single linear sequence ending with the Final Challenge: `Defeat the Kalos Champion at Kalos Pokemon League`.
- **Starter Extra Challenge Chain:** This chain (`Kiloude City -> Friend Safari -> Battle Maison -> Starter Depot Spots`) unlocks *specifically* alongside the reward for defeating the 4th Gym (`Coumarine City Gym`).
- **Post-League Extra Challenges:** After defeating the Kalos Champion, 3 parallel chains of Extra Challenges unlock simultaneously:
    - **Chain B:** `Sea Spirit Den Entrance -> Sea Spirit Den Tunnels -> Sea Spirit Den Hall`
    - **Chain C:** `Chambers of Emptiness -> Team Flare Secret HQ Entrance -> Team Flare Secret HQ Hall`
    - **Chain D:** Completely renamed routes ending in the Ultimate Chamber (`Mystic Tower Entrance -> Crystal Cave Tunnels -> Secret Forest North -> Ultimate Chamber`)
- **Wording:** Every challenge explicitly details the `Unlock [Next Spot]` in the Rewards column.

## 3. Missing Pokemon (Sheet 3)
- **Controlled Limitations:** Exactly 40 evolved forms were selected to be the "Missing Pokemon". The remaining ~417 forms (including all Base stages and Legendaries) were mapped explicitly into the game on Sheet 1.
- **Obtainability:** Because all Base stages are available to catch, every single missing Pokemon in this sheet can be obtained via evolution. Therefore, the `Obtained?` column is set to `Yes` for all 40 entries.
